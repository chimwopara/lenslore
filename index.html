About Me

I'm Kiitan Kujembola, an Edmonton-based branding photographer focused on creating intentional visuals that help people and businesses communicate credibility and clarity. Rather than just making images look good, I approach photography as a strategic tool that supports how a brand is perceived.

With a background in finance at the University of Alberta, I bring a business-first mindset to every project. Each shoot starts with understanding the audience, the message, and the outcome the brand is aiming for.

My role goes beyond taking photos. I help guide posing, composition, and visual direction so images feel consistent, purposeful, and aligned with how they'll be used across websites, social media, and marketing.
